# Margarita

Glass: Chilled Martini / Coupe
Ice: None
Method: Shake & Fine Strain
Garnish: Salt Rim (Full Half or None)
Taste: Good
Base: Tequila
Recipe Source: EBS Inter

# Ingredients

|  | MLS |
| --- | --- |
| Tequila | 40 |
| Triple Sec | 20 |
| Fresh Lime Juice | 30 |
| Sugar Syrup | 10 |
